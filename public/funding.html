Funding and community impact

Where outside funding would make the biggest difference, and how funders and partners can reach us.

Growth priorities

  • Vehicle readiness. Inspected, accessible vehicles so trips happen on time.
  • Driver training. Train local drivers for passenger, medical-transport and courier work.
  • Dispatch capacity. Scheduling that can take on more trips without missed pickups.
  • Local mobility. Getting seniors and people without a car to appointments, dialysis and therapy.

Planned outcomes

We set targets for each funded project, such as drivers trained, vehicles ready for service and trips completed. These are targets, not past results, and we report against them.

Applications, budgets and supporting records are shared privately with funders, not published here.
