How a request is handled

The same steps for a single courier run or a multi-site contract.

  1. You send the scope

    Locations, timing, volumes, and any solicitation number or link.

  2. We confirm fit

    Service area, schedule, vehicles and any special handling.

  3. You get a written answer

    A quote, a teaming reply, or a clear no.

  4. Service starts

    On the agreed schedule, with records kept for each job.
