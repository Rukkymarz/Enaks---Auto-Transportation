Privacy

How we handle information sent through this website.

What we collect

The enquiry form asks for your name, phone, email, the service you need, the location, and details you choose to share. Organization, dates and solicitation numbers are optional.

How we use it

Only to review and reply to your enquiry. Sending an enquiry does not confirm a reservation or service.

How it travels

When you send the form, your details pass through our website host and email-delivery provider to our inbox. We do not publish submissions or sell them. Those providers may keep technical or delivery records under their own policies.

Sensitive information

Do not include diagnoses, medical records, insurance or patient ID numbers, or payment card details. The form blocks common health details. This website is not a patient portal or an emergency channel. For emergencies, call 911.

Your choices

To correct or delete information you sent us, call (404) 354-7930 or email Eautotransport@mail.com.
